## What the OperatorFlow will do at 06:00 ET

1. **morning_review** — runs `review_all_active_strategies()` which computes rolling 20-trade PF / expectancy / max consec losers / days-since-last-fire for each of the 21 active strategies. Calls MiniMax M2.7 per strategy with deterministic fallback. ~3–5 min wall clock.
2. **classify** routes the result:
   - If any strategy hit retire criteria → `retire_step` calls `retire_strategy()` (atomic, with rollback preserved in `auto_demotions`)
   - If today is Saturday/Sunday ET, OR no turnover in 7 days, OR regime shifted, OR brief had surprises → `explore_step` plans + dispatches a research experiment (M2.7 picks tool/symbol/params, deterministic fallback if LLM fails, 10-min hard timeout)
   - Else → `write_brief_step`
3. **write_brief_step** always runs — composes daily brief from `trades` + regime + auto_demotions + surprises, persists to `daily_briefs` + `outputs/briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, surfaced on dashboard.

If anything fails, the flow swallows the error, logs it, and falls through to the brief — never crashes the daily run.
